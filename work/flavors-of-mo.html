Flavors of MO

  • ClientMandarin Oriental Hotel
  • Year2024
  • AgencyTCU
  • CountryQatar

Challenge

Mandarin Oriental, Doha, wanted a special way to show off their amazing food and bring in top chefs and food lovers. They needed an exciting event and brand that highlighted different cultures through cooking, making their hotel the best place for unique food experiences.

Solution

We created “Flavors of MO,” a unique cooking event series. It’s all about celebrating food from around the world with live cooking shows and delicious tastings. This helps the hotel stand out as a place for exciting and diverse culinary adventures, bringing together great chefs and guests through the love of food.
