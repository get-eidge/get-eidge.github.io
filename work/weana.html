Challenge

In an increasingly connected world, the challenge was to simplify networking and contact sharing, making it seamless, smart, and universally accessible. Traditional business cards often get lost or outdated. Weana needed a brand identity and product design for their NFC-enabled smart cards that would clearly communicate innovation, ease of use, and efficiency. The goal was to establish these cards as a modern, stylish, and essential tool for professionals who value quick, reliable connections without the hassle of physical paper cards or complex digital setups.

Solution

We developed the brand identity and design for Weana Smart Cards, focusing on creating a vibrant, modern, and user-friendly image. These innovative NFC-enabled cards are designed to instantly share contact details, social media profiles, and portfolios with just one tap—no apps or QR codes needed. The solution emphasizes real-time connection in seconds, making networking effortless. The stylishly branded cards, featuring Weana’s vibrant identity and signature data, are positioned not just as a tool but as an extension of the user, embodying the idea that “every connection matters” and can be made “together connected, one tap, like a snap!”
