Doha Bank

  • ClientDoha Bank
  • Year2024
  • AgencyTCU
  • CountryQatar

Challenge

Doha Bank sought a modern digital-first brand identity that still honored its heritage. The core challenge was to create a fresh, forward-thinking look that would appeal to a new generation of digital-savvy customers while reassuring existing clients. Importantly, the client wanted to maintain a connection to their past, specifically by incorporating or echoing the iconic shape of the Doha Bank building from their old logo within the new design. This required a delicate balance between innovation and tradition.

Solution

We developed a new brand identity for Doha Bank, featuring a bold, digital-first aesthetic. The visual system is inspired by simplicity, geometry, and motion, embodying trust and progress. Critically, the new logo design subtly integrates the distinctive shape of the Doha Bank building, providing a direct link to the bank’s legacy and original identity. The design uses clear curves and structured grids to create a cohesive and recognizable brand presence across all touchpoints, from mobile apps to physical signage, reinforcing the bank’s image as a reliable and innovative leader in the digital age.
