Rajh Endowment

  • ClientAlrajihi
  • Year2023
  • AgencyGenerator
  • CountrySaudi Arabia

Challenge

Rajh Endowment, a significant institution under AlRajhi, sought to modernize its brand identity while retaining its core essence and established recognition. The key challenge was to refresh their existing brand by updating its typography to a more contemporary style and revitalizing the color palette. This refresh needed to make the brand feel more modern and relevant without alienating its long-standing audience, meaning the new colors had to stay close to the traditional hues while gaining a refreshed, more vibrant feel. The goal was to signal innovation and continued relevance while honoring its heritage and trustworthiness.

Solution

We developed a refreshed brand identity for Rajh Endowment, carefully balancing modernization with tradition. The solution focused on a comprehensive update of the brand’s typography, introducing a more modern and legible font that enhanced readability and conveyed a contemporary image. Simultaneously, the color palette was revitalized; while staying close to the original, established colors, we introduced fresher, more vibrant shades that gave the brand a renewed sense of energy and sophistication. This subtle yet impactful redesign allowed Rajh Endowment to project a more current image, signaling forward-thinking progress while maintaining the familiar and trusted visual cues that resonate with its audience.
