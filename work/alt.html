07 / 17 — Branding

ALT

  • ClientALT
  • Year2026

Challenge

In a world where music is everywhere, ALT — a platform built for independent artists and their fans — needed a brand that is felt, not just seen. It had to hold its own next to global streaming giants while speaking the bold, fearless language of a new generation of Arab artists.

Solution

We created an identity that behaves like a movement: a pixel-stepped ALT wordmark, an electric palette led by hot pink with high-contrast black and vivid accents, and a bilingual type system pairing Codec Pro Arabic with TT Hoves Pro. Authentic imagery of real artists and a series of statement posters — “Break the Norm”, “Glitch the System” — bring the attitude to life.
